Support

We got you

Common questions, answered.

How does the Personal (zero-based) budgeting mode work here?

Every dollar of income gets assigned to a category until your "Remaining" stat hits zero. The Beast Meter fills as you allocate.

Can I export to Excel or Numbers?

Yes — use "Export CSV" on the Budget page. CSV files open cleanly in both Microsoft Excel and Apple Numbers.

How do I get a PDF?

Click "Print / PDF" and choose "Save as PDF" in your browser's print dialog.

Can I add my own categories?

Yes — use the "Add Category" field at the bottom of your budget, and "+ Add Line Item" inside any category.

Is my data saved?

This preview keeps data in memory for your session. When you host this yourself, connect a database or browser storage for persistence — see the code comments near STATE.

What's the difference between Personal and Business budgeting?

Personal mode uses zero-based budgeting for household finances. Business mode swaps in Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold, Payroll, Operating Expenses, and Taxes categories, and unlocks the Profit & Loss Snapshot, the 12-Month P&L view, and CPA-ready printing.

Will I lose my data if I switch between Personal and Business budgeting?

Switching budget types resets your categories to that type's template, so you'll see a confirmation prompt first if you already have data entered. Your Debts, income sources, and account name are not affected by the switch.

Does my budget carry over from month to month?

Yes — use the month arrows next to "Refresh" on the Budget page. Each month you visit is saved with its own numbers, and a new month automatically carries forward your category structure while resetting "Actual" back to $0 so you start clean.

What do the Beginner, Advanced, and Expert skill levels do?

The skill selector in the header changes how much detail you see in Beastmode Money Tips and in your Insights — Beginner keeps guidance simple, while Advanced and Expert unlock deeper strategy like avalanche vs. snowball debt payoff comparisons and ratio-based analysis.

How does the free trial work?

Starting the Starter plan gives you full Beastmode Business access for 30 days, shown as a countdown banner under the header. It automatically converts to the Beastmode plan when the trial ends, or the banner disappears immediately if you sign up for Beastmode or Beastmode Business directly.

(Site owner) How do I turn on real accounts?

The Sign In button in the header is ready to go — it just needs a free Supabase project connected. Create one, copy your Project URL and anon key from Project Settings → API, and paste them into the SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_ANON_KEY constants near the top of the site's script (search for "ACCOUNTS: SUPABASE AUTH"). Add your Vercel URL under Authentication → URL Configuration so confirmation and password-reset emails link back correctly. No server code is needed for accounts themselves — Supabase's SDK talks directly and securely from the browser. Note that signing in is separate from having an active paid plan; gating access behind a real Stripe payment needs a couple of small server functions, since your Stripe secret key can never live in this file.
