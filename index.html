Support

We got you

Common questions, answered.

How does Personal (zero-based) budgeting work here?

Every dollar of income gets assigned to a category until your "Remaining" stat hits zero. The Beast Meter fills as you allocate, so you can see at a glance how close you are to giving every dollar a job.

What's the difference between Personal and Business budgeting?

Personal mode uses zero-based budgeting for household finances. Business mode swaps in Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold, Payroll, Operating Expenses, and Taxes categories, and unlocks the Profit & Loss Snapshot, the 12-Month P&L view, and CPA-ready printing.

Can I add my own categories and line items?

Yes — use the "Add Category" field at the bottom of your budget, and "+ Add Line Item" inside any category. You can rename or remove any item anytime, and Beastmode Money Tips adjust automatically to match what you type.

How do I export or print my budget?

Use "Export CSV" for a file that opens cleanly in Excel or Numbers, or "Print / PDF" for a clean report (choose "Save as PDF" in your browser's print dialog). On the Business plan, the Profit & Loss Snapshot also has its own "Print P&L Only" button and a printable 12-Month view.

Is my budget data saved between visits?

Yes — once you're signed in, your categories, income, debts, and settings save automatically to your account and are there waiting for you the next time you sign in, on any device.

Does my budget carry over from month to month?

Yes — use the month arrows next to "Refresh" on the Budget page. Each month you visit carries forward your category structure while resetting "Actual" back to $0, so you always start the month clean, and it's all saved to your account.

What do the Beginner, Advanced, and Expert skill levels do?

The skill selector in the header changes how much detail you see in Beastmode Money Tips and in your Insights — Beginner keeps guidance simple, while Advanced and Expert unlock deeper strategy like avalanche vs. snowball debt payoff comparisons and ratio-based analysis.

How do Beastmode Money Tips work?

Click the yellow badge next to any line item for a tip tailored to that specific expense (rent, groceries, payroll, and dozens more each have their own advice) and to your selected skill level. Acknowledging a tip also refreshes the rotating "Beastmode Insight" card on the Insights page with something new.

How does the free trial work, and how do I manage or cancel my plan?

Choosing the Starter plan gives you full Beastmode Business access for 30 days, shown as a countdown banner under the header, and automatically converts to the Beastmode plan if you don't cancel. To manage, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime, use the "Manage my plan" button — it opens Stripe's secure billing portal.

How do I retake the plan quiz, or change my currency or language?

On the Home page, click "Retake questions" under your recommendation to run the quiz again. The currency and language selectors sit at the bottom of the "Let's find your plan" box — currency changes the symbol shown across the site, and language translates the site's menus, pages and buttons.
