Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

The short version: we do not open, read or review the budget information you enter, and we do not sell it or share it with advertisers. It is saved to your private account so it is there when you sign in, and only your signed-in account can read it.

This policy explains what Beastmode Budget (the "Service," at beastmodebudget.com) collects, why, and the choices you have. "We," "us" and "our" mean Crown Life Enterprises, LLC, which owns and operates the Service.

What we collect

WhatWhy we have it
Account details: your email address and password (the password is handled by our sign-in provider; we cannot see it)To create your account and let you sign in on any device
Your budget information: income, categories, amounts, debts, savings goals, paychecks, settings, and the name you give the appOnly to show it back to you whenever you sign in
Plan and billing status: your plan, trial dates and subscription statusTo unlock the right features and manage your subscription
Plan-finder quiz answers: your first name, location, email and answers, if you take the quiz on the Home pageTo recommend a plan and so we can follow up with you
Messages you send us: the Contact form or emailTo reply to you
Basic technical data: browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate region, from server logs and, if we turn it on, simple website analyticsTo keep the site working, secure, and improve it. Analytics never include your budget entries.

We do not collect your bank logins or account numbers. Beastmode Budget does not connect to your bank.

Your budget data is yours

We do not open, read or review the numbers and names you enter into your budget, and we do not use them for advertising. Like any hosted service, the data is stored with our infrastructure providers (below), and technical access is limited to running, securing and supporting the Service, for example fixing a problem you ask us to help with, or when the law requires it.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We receive your plan, subscription status and billing contact information from Stripe.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users' safety, or in a business transfer (such as a sale of the company), in which case this policy's protections continue to apply.

Cookies and device storage

We use your browser's storage to keep you signed in and to keep a quick copy of your budget on your own device so the site loads fast. We also remember things like your language, currency and whether you finished the quiz. We do not use advertising cookies. You can clear this storage in your browser settings; you may then need to sign in again.

How we protect it

Connections to the Service are encrypted (HTTPS). Your saved budget is protected by account-level access rules so that only your signed-in account can read or change it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and to fix problems promptly.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and budget data while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your budget data within 30 days, except records we must keep for taxes, fraud prevention or legal reasons (for example, payment records held by Stripe).

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can see and edit your budget data anytime by signing in.
  • Deletion: email us from your account email and ask us to delete your account and data.
  • Email: you can unsubscribe from any marketing email using the link inside it. We will still send essential account and billing messages.
  • Cancel anytime: use the Manage my plan button on the Budget page.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights (for example, in California). Contact us and we will honor valid requests.

Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you on the site or by email.

Contact us

Questions or requests: use the Contact page on the site or email crownlifeseminars@gmail.com.
